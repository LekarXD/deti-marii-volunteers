                               Life Value Terms

     Life Value Terms are short phrases with shared meaning that can be used in daily
interactions with children and youth. Life Value Terms differ from life skills, in that they give
caregivers and children language to use for understanding, communicating, and learning
    valuable skills. Essentially, Life Value Terms are the language of a trauma-informed
    culture. This language not only helps children use their voice in an appropriate and
   respectful manner, but it also provides caregivers with tools for engaging children in
                         healthy communication within relationships.

                                    “Accepting ‘No’”

                          When some children hear "no," they
                          believe that their needs will never be
                          met, and that they have no voice or
                          value. Caregivers can get creative
                          about ways to give more "yeses" than
                          "nos" so children learn that they are
                          heard and valued. When "no" is
                          necessary, however, praise children for
                          accepting "no".
